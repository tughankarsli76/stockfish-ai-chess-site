Support

Stockfish AI Chess by Tughan Y. Karsli

Email tughankarsli@gmail.com for help. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot or PGN can help; remove any information you do not want to share.

Choose an engine

Open Options → Change Engine. Stockfish 19, Stockfish 18, Reckless 0.9.0, PlentyChess 8.0.0, Pawnocchio 2.0.1, and Obsidian 16.0 are included. All work without an internet connection.

Engine matches

Open Game → New Game → Engine Match. Choose each engine and its time per move. Use the Game menu to pause, resume, or stop. Opening menus pauses a match.

Analysis and strength

Stockfish engines support adjustable playing strength. Other engines play at full strength. Pawnocchio displays one analysis line; other included engines support multiple lines. WDL percentages appear only when supported by the engine or opening data.

Save and transfer games

Use Game → Save Game or Export Game. PGN files can be imported through the iOS document picker. Public-game imports from Chess.com and Lichess require internet access and an existing public username.

Warm device or battery use

Long analysis uses the processor. Select one thread and a shorter analysis time in Options, or stop analysis when finished. Engine searches stop when the app becomes inactive.

Board appearance

Options → Board includes Honey Wood and other colors. Change the chess pieces separately under Options → Pieces.

Independent app

This app is independently developed and is not an official Stockfish, Chess.com, or Lichess app.
